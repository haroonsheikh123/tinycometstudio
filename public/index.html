About us

A small team with a big soft spot for play

Tiny Comet Studio is an independent team based in Pakistan, founded by Haroon Sheikh.

We started Tiny Comet because we love the feeling of a great little game or a genuinely useful app — the kind you open on the bus, smile at, and come back to tomorrow. We're small on purpose: it keeps us close to every detail, and close to the people who play what we make.

Everything we build is designed, coded and polished in-house, and we share the journey as we go. If you like what you see, come say hi — we read every message.
